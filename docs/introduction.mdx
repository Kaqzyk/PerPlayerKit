PerPlayerKit solves one of the most common frustrations on PvP servers: the one-size-fits-all kit. Rather than forcing every player into a preset loadout, PerPlayerKit gives each player their own kit builder. You configure a Virtual Kit Room stocked with the items you approve, and players freely choose from that pool to assemble their saved kits. Every kit is stored per-player, sharable with friends, and fully inspectable by staff — giving you both flexibility and control.

## Anti-Exploit Protection

PerPlayerKit includes a built-in anti-exploit layer that prevents players from injecting unauthorized items into their kits. NBT data and enchantments are validated against vanilla Minecraft limits, and you can optionally restrict kits to items that exist in your Virtual Kit Room. Tune all of it in `config.yml` under the [`anti-exploit`](/configuration/anti-exploit) section.

## Requirements

Before installing PerPlayerKit, confirm your environment meets these minimum requirements:

| Requirement       | Minimum Version |
| ----------------- | --------------- |
| Server software   | Paper or Spigot |
| Minecraft version | 1.19 or newer   |
| Java              | 17 or newer     |

### Optional Integrations

Both are soft dependencies — PerPlayerKit runs fine without either.

| Plugin                                                                              | What it adds                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [PlaceholderAPI](https://www.spigotmc.org/resources/placeholderapi.6245/)           | Placeholders in plugin messages.                                                                  |
| [WorldGuard](https://enginehub.org/worldguard)                                      | Region-specific kits for [rekit-on-kill](/configuration/rekit-on-kill).                           |
